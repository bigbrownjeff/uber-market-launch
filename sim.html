The supply-first wedge

The demand side gets the press release; the supply side gets the late-night Slack thread when the Tuesday courier fill-rate drops below the merchant SLA. The free-rider column is the one most experimental-design write-ups skip — it tells you whether the incentive is buying behaviour or buying a payroll bump for people already on the road.
